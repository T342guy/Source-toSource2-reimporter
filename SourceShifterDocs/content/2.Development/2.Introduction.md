This part of the SourceShifter documentation will purely be about the development side of SourceShifter.\
Itll shift around from issues, problems, solutions and in-depth overviews of how the current system works (when we make it).

SourceShifter is by no means a small scale project. But as a VALVe fan and SFM user myself (T342guy), I hope that the community will have a better time moving to S2FM, and have one less burden against the community when SourceShifter does come to life.

## The current SourceShifter plan 

The current SourceShfiter plan is a bit all over the place. Assumptions were made that other things would have this compatability, this thing we didnt expect, so fourth.\
If you check the commit history on the `SDD.md` file, it might be clear that I wasnt looking at a whole lot.

That is also very clear with the legacy ideas, as those were the first I had on how the porting pipeline would work for SourceShifter. And unsurprisingly, the ideas were factually incorrect.
